Coaching philosophy

Think for yourself,
grow through practice.

Training for the Japan Sport Association Coach IV certification led me to rethink my coaching in 2019. I used to set every drill and focus on creating a tough environment. Now I value having players think for themselves and grow by taking the initiative.

We often adopt drills the players design themselves. Clear rules keep practice focused and build patience and social skills. Older players teach younger ones, which sharpens their ability to explain and deepens their own understanding. Less about teaching, more about players learning.

Head Coach Takamasa Harada
