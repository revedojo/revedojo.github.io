Philosophy

Dignified character,
proven by unshakable strength.

At RÊVE DOJO, what matters most is the respect and gratitude you show the opponent across the table. The focus of putting your whole spirit into every ball, and the grace of each movement, embody the Japanese aesthetics of sport.

While our junior team competes at national level year after year, we also teach adults who have never held a racket, building real technique from zero with clear, logical coaching. Step inside and you will stand a little taller. This is a proud table tennis dojo.

Strength to compete nationally

A team that keeps competing on the national stage: National Hopes boys top 8, JOC Junior Olympic qualifiers, TOKYO OPEN and more.
